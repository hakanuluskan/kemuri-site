FAQ

How do I change the app language?

Settings → Language. Kemuri supports English, 日本語 and Türkçe; it does not follow the phone language automatically.

How does verification work?

Tap a spot, then “Still here” or “It's gone” — only when you are actually near it (we check your GPS distance). Enough “gone” reports from different people retire community spots; official spots can only be flagged as suspicious and are re-checked against the official source.

A spot is wrong, duplicated or inappropriate.

Open the spot and tap “Report a problem with this spot”, or email us the name and location. We review reports and remove content that breaks the Terms of Use.

How do I restore or cancel Premium?

Restore: Premium screen → “Restore purchases” (no account needed; it is tied to your Apple ID / Google Play account). Cancel: iPhone — Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions; Android — Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
